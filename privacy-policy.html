Privacy Policy - FormatHaven

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how FormatHaven (the "App") handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any information, including personal information. The App contains no analytics, no advertising, and no telemetry. It requires no account and works fully offline. The App is completely free: it shows no advertisements and contains no in-app purchases.

2. What the App Stores and Where

The App formats and cleans up text you enter or paste. Only your app settings and your customizations of the formatting rules are stored, in the App's own local data folder on your PC. The text you enter is processed only on your device; it is never saved by the App and never sent to any external server or third party. The App makes no network connections.

3. How to Delete Your Data

Uninstalling the App removes its stored data from your PC.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

The App collects no information, so there is nothing to share with third parties.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact: kasayuki05022@gmail.com

6. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
